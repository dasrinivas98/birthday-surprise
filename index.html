the process

From memory to gift, in three steps

You bring the story. We wrap it in design. It's a real back-and-forth, not a form you fill and forget.

01
💌

Tell us the story

Names, the date, the photos that matter, the inside jokes. A short call or a message thread — whatever's easier for you.

02
🎁

We wrap it, just for you

A layout, colours, and words built around your occasion — not a theme picked off a shelf. You see drafts before anything is final.

03
💝

You share the gift

One page, one link, sent however you like — WhatsApp, a card, or read aloud in person. It's live the moment you say go.
